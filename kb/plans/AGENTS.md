# Contents

- Plans record proposed, active, blocked, superseded, and completed implementation or migration work.

# Guidelines

- Record the requested outcome, scope, dependencies, decisions, phases, verification evidence, deviations, review findings, and terminal result.
- Keep one owner for convergence files and make parallel lanes explicit.
- Move durable conclusions into `kb/notes/` when a plan reaches a terminal state.
- Do not mark a plan complete until its required evidence and external delivery state are verified.
